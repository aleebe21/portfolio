How I work a dataset

Electric Vehicle Infrastructure Forecasting
Python · Pandas · scikit-learn — May 2025

Washington State has more EVs than it has places to charge them, but the shortfall isn't spread evenly. I merged the state's full vehicle registration file with its charging station inventory to find where the gap was widest — and found the worst of it in the suburbs, not the cities everyone was watching.

239K+
EV registrations merged
2,907
Charging stations mapped
0.94
Adoption–access correlation
1,200:1
Worst EV-to-charger ratio, vs. 55:1 in Seattle

The output was a shortlist: 7 high-priority suburban sites across 502 cities where new infrastructure would serve the most drivers per dollar.
